# Code of Conduct

We want Blockscope to be a friendly, relaxed place where people feel safe to ask
questions and contribute. This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and
project chat.

- Respect different experience levels and points of view; critique code, not people.
- Keep questions, reviews, and bug reports patient and constructive.
- Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and sharing private
  information are not welcome.
- If you accidentally upset someone, be willing to apologize and adjust.
- If something feels off, contact the maintainer
  [@Linvin-1233](https://github.com/Linvin-1233); reports will be handled
  discreetly.
